# Assessment evidence checks

Checked 16 November 2026. These are record checks for an assessment office, not accreditation, proctoring or certification. The default policy is informed by the Australian NVR RTO standards. It is not a statement that every organisation, New Zealand provider or qualification has these obligations.

Source: [ASQA assessment practice guide, updated 29 October 2026](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-providers/standards-for-RTOs/practice-guides/quality-area-1-training-and-assessment/assessment-practice-guide).

| Rule | Implementation | Basis and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Q01 | Every question revision needs a different named reviewer, evidence and a future review date. | Standard 1.3 requires fit-for-purpose review. Separate author and reviewer and the chosen expiry are local controls, not prescribed legal deadlines. |
| A01 | Flag missing questions, stale question revisions and uncovered blueprint marks. | Local content-control check supporting fit-for-purpose review. Marks do not establish coverage of all competency requirements. |
| R01 | Results remain unreviewed until a person records validity, sufficiency, authenticity, currency and evidence. Acceptance requires all four. | Standard 1.4 evidence principles. A tick and a note are not proof that the evidence is adequate. The assessor inspects the underlying work. Score thresholds never issue qualifications. |
| V01 | A programme needs a validation record, credential reference and next date. The CLI caps the next date at five years. | Standard 1.5 requires at least five-year validation and earlier reviews when risks arise. The independent named lead is a local minimum, not a credential verification service. Special TAE qualification requirements need further configuration. |
| V02 | A recorded risk remains visible even after a validation decision. | Standard 1.5 calls for risk-based review. The operator determines the scope, sampling and earlier deadline. |

Evidence decisions and source results are append-only through the application. The most recent moderation or question review governs the current view. Question revision changes require fresh approval and a deliberate update to each assessment that uses it. Older wording is retained in the change history.

Author and reviewer names are audit labels, not authenticated identities. The application cannot establish independence or credentials from names. A database owner can alter these controls. Use restricted operator roles and a backup/restore policy for shared use. There is no public Data API access grant.

No automatic evidence deletion or retention promise is included. Set the retention schedule against the provider's actual obligations and contracts. Protect learner identifiers and marking guides. Use fictional data in demos. Do not treat a clean checklist as a legal conclusion.
